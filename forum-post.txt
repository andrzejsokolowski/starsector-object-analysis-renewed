[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
The Objects Analysis ability from Harpuea's Objects Analysis, without its frame rate cost.
[/b][/center]
[center]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]The Ability[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Hover Objects Analysis on your ability bar to list the objects in the current star system that a scan could pick up: stations and neutrino sources.[/li]
[li]It does not say where they are.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]What Changed[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]The original scanned the whole star system every frame to check whether the ability could be switched on. It never can. With many objects in a system that cost about 7% of every frame on the campaign map.[/li]
[li]Now nothing runs until you hover the ability, and the list is built with far fewer searches.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Replaces Objects Analysis: turn that mod off. With both on, the game stops at start and tells you.[/li]
[li]Same ability, so it keeps its place on your ability bar.[/li]
[li]Safe to add to or remove from a game in progress.[/li]
[/list]
